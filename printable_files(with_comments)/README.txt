--> Generally speaking, print slow with a low-medium flowrate and with very little cooling fan. This way, the printed parts will be strong because the layers adhere very well to each other.
--> Set "seam postiion" to random. This way all bearing fits are most concentric. With an alligned seam position, the concentricity of the bearing fit might be off.

I printed everything on a Sovol SV07 with a .6-nozzle at 5 mm^3/s max flow rate and 0-20 % part cooling fan.
